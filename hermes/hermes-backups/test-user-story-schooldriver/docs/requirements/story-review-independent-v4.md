## 1. Verdict

**CONDITIONAL PASS — Gate 1 MAY PROCEED; do NOT lock baseline v1.0 as-is until the conditions in §5 are applied in this same revision cycle.**

Nothing blocks Gate 1. All prior dispositions from v1 (IR-01…18), v2 (V2-01…11), and v3 (F1…F7) are **genuinely closed in the current files** (re-verified line-by-line, including the two v3 items the history itself flagged as pending: F4 → D-27 + US-05 AC-5, and F6 → D-28 — both present and internally consistent). The amendment (D-29…D-33, CR-21…24, US-01…08 AC updates, OQ-16…18, A-13/A-14, D-06 annotation) is largely coherent: gender rule, car purity, service areas, time containment, direction independence, and the per-shift model are mutually consistent with CR-04 and with the surviving single-objective/exact-optimality decisions.

However, "the noon = reversed morning concept is fully removed" (pass-12's claim) is **false**: the decision record D-21 still carries the live phrase "minimum total **morning** driving distance" (V4-06). More importantly, a fresh adversarial pass over the new model finds **3 Major ambiguities the amendment introduced but did not resolve** — all in the new per-shift/per-direction matching core:

1. **DriverShift semantics** (V4-01): the driver availability entity is defined as "school shift + direction flags + time window" (CR-03) while the school is simultaneously declared "not predefined — determined by matching" (CR-04, US-03 AC-4). The artifact permits two mutually contradictory readings and no OQ/decision pins one.
2. **Feasibility is not defined per direction** (V4-02): CR-06/US-04 AC-1 define feasibility "per school and per shift" only, yet the assignment obligation is per direction with direction-specific driver pools (flags, windows). This is the IR-01 defect class recurring in the new model's central guarantee.
3. **From-school run geometry is undefined** (V4-03): D-24 fixes route start = driver home, but the from-school run's start (school vs origin), deadhead accounting, and per-student pickup order/time semantics for the from-school direction are unstated — the distance objective (CR-07, the *only* objective) and the US-07 roster content depend on them.

Plus 5 more Minor findings and 3 Notes (D-21 stale text, US-05 matrix gap, OQ-18/AC-2 coupling, US-06 per-direction scope, missing CR-23 ACs, US-02 edit-path validation, post-approval shift edits, Rule-7 wording tokens, NFR-08 delta visibility, CR-18 eligibility guidance). None invalidates an acceptance criterion as written; three need product-owner decisions before baseline (V4-01, V4-03, and the OQ-18 confirmation in V4-07), the rest are mechanical.

**Conditions for baseline (v1.0):**
1. PO decisions on V4-01 (DriverShift semantics), V4-03 (from-school route start/order/times), and V4-07 (OQ-18 confirmation → record as a decision).
2. Mechanical fixes: V4-02 (CR-06/AC-1 wording), V4-04 (matrix D-27), V4-06 (D-21 wording), V4-08 (US-06 note), V4-09 (time-rule AC), V4-10 (US-02 AC-4 wording), V4-11 (OQ-10 extension or decision), V4-12/V4-13/V4-14 (text notes).
