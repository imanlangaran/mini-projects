## 1. Verdict

**CONDITIONAL PASS — DO NOT BASELINE AS-IS (hold Gate 1 approval).**

The package is well-structured, traceable, and unusually disciplined for this stage: 5 of the 20 confirmed requirements and both major story-level risk areas flagged by the prior reviewer were fixed correctly (verified below). However, a fresh adversarial pass finds **5 Major and 13 Minor defects** the prior review history missed — including two that make the *core acceptance criteria unsound* (IR-01: US-04's feasibility precondition is mathematically wrong under the one-school-per-driver rule; IR-05: the primary success metric SM-01 contradicts the Must-priority exclusion feature) and two that need product-owner decisions before baseline (IR-02 distance semantics with budget implications, IR-03 optimality-at-scale guarantee). The prior history's "zero open findings" (pass 5) is therefore **challenged**: those passes closed what they found, but the package was not clean.

Recommended sequence: (a) author applies the mechanical fixes (IR-01 wording in US-04 AC-1/AC-6 + CR-06; IR-05 SM-01 wording); (b) product owner answers Q1–Q9 (below) — IR-02/IR-03/IR-04 are baseline blockers, the rest can be recorded as decisions/assumptions; (c) focused re-review of the delta; then human approval.

## 4. Questions for the product owner

1. **Distance semantics (IR-02, blocker):** Is "driving distance" straight-line or road-network? May the solution use an external geocoding/routing service, and is its cost compatible with the NFR-08 $30/month operating ceiling? Where does each route start (driver origin) — and is driver origin/school location part of registration data?
2. **Optimality guarantee (IR-03, blocker):** At production scale (500 students/50 drivers), is exact optimality required, or is a documented heuristic with a stated quality bound acceptable (exact only on small instances)?
3. **Driver record edits (IR-04, blocker):** May a driver's capacity be reduced below their current assignment count on an approved/published plan? What should the system do when it is (reject / block / invalidate the plan)?
4. **Success metric (IR-05):** Confirm SM-01 should exclude flagged students and evaluate CR-18 failure runs on report completeness rather than coverage.
5. **Plan lifecycle (IR-11):** While a newer matching report awaits approval and an older plan is already published, what do parents/drivers see? If the admin triggers an occasional re-run, do US-06 tweaks survive it?
6. **Mid-year flag/removal (IR-12):** When a student is flagged or removed after publication, should the change propagate to live rosters/parent views immediately, at the next run, or via an explicit admin tweak?
7. **Vehicle identity requiredness (IR-09):** Are plate/model/color all mandatory at driver registration? Are duplicate plates rejected?
8. **Location fields (IR-07/IR-08):** What exactly is captured for school "location" and student "home location/address" (address, coordinates, both), and which is required? (Resolves the carried slash-ambiguity.)
9. **Driver person identity (IR-14):** What identifies "the driver" to the parent (name only? contact details?) and what fields are captured at registration?
10. **Parent credentials (hidden assumption, CR-20/A-10 area):** How do parents with auto-created accounts receive sign-in credentials (SMS/email/printed handout)? This delivery path is currently unstated.
11. **Duplicate definition (IR-09/IR-18, NFR-09):** What makes a school/student/driver a "duplicate registration" (name+address? same plate? school-provided ID)?
12. **Student identity (IR-18):** What stable identifier distinguishes students for registry, reassignment, flagging, and parent↔student linkage (A-10)?
13. **OQ-06 timing (existing open item):** Confirm OQ-06 (one-school-per-driver under manual reassignment) remains open but non-blocking, and that it must be closed before US-06 is baselined. — (Agree with current handling; needs explicit confirmation at approval.)
