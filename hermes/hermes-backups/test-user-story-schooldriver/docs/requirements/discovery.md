## 1. Product Intent (Phase 0 input, approved)

A smart transportation platform that efficiently matches drivers with users based on location, time, capacity, and destination. It optimizes pickup routes to minimize total driving distance while ensuring all users are assigned.

**Target users (confirmed during session):**

| Role | Who they are | System interaction |
|---|---|---|
| Student (passenger) | Child transported daily | Rides only; does **not** use the dashboard (parent does) |
| Parent/Guardian | Checks the dashboard | Views their child's assignment; primary need: **driver/vehicle identity** |
| Driver | Provides transport | Sees roster (students, order, times) via dashboard and exported file |
| Administrator | School staff | Registers schools/students/drivers, triggers matching, reviews/publishes report, tweaks assignments |

**Confirmed: no other stakeholders** (no school secretary, coordinator, or regulator in the loop).

## 2. Confirmed Requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| CR-01 | The system shall support multiple schools; each school is registered by an administrator and has a **location — address and coordinates, both required** (D-26), a **sex (single-gender: male or female, D-29/D-30)**, and **one or more shifts**, each with a start time and end time (D-33). |
| CR-02 | Each student shall be registered with a **national ID (کد ملی)** as the **unique student key** — **10-digit, format-validated, unique across the registry** (OQ-13 → D-52) — a **home location — address and coordinates, both required** (D-26) — tied to exactly one school, with a **sex equal to the school's sex (validated, D-30)**, and **exactly one shift selected from the school's shifts** (D-33). The home address and map-selected location are provided by the **parent/guardian** (D-28); the **student registration is initiated by the parent** and becomes a student record when the **administrator/school accepts** it (OQ-15 → D-53, mirrors the CR-20 parent-request pattern). Registry, reassignment (US-06), flagging (US-09), and export key off the **کد ملی** — stable across school changes (D-52). |
| CR-03 | Each driver's vehicle capacity shall be recorded in the system; capacities may differ between drivers. The driver's record shall also include vehicle identity details — **plate number, model, and color** (OQ-05, resolved; editable after publication, D-25) — plus the **driver's name and phone number**, shown to parents (OQ-12 → D-51), and the driver's **national ID (کد ملی)** (duplicate-check key — OQ-13 → D-52) — sufficient for a parent to recognize the vehicle **and the driver**, the driver's **origin (route start): home address, captured at registration** (D-24), the driver's **sex (male or female, D-29)**, **one or more service areas** (**predefined zones**, D-31/D-43), and **service availability for one or more system-defined shifts** (DriverShift: **selected shift + to-school/from-school flags** — the driver marks pickup (at shift start) and/or drop-off (at shift end), one or both, per selected shift, and may select **multiple shifts**; the entry names **no school** — school-agnostic, D-35). Per-direction service windows derive from the selected shifts (D-42 — explicit sub-windows deferred post-MVP; D-32/D-33 remain the direction/time-model basis). |
| CR-04 | **No school is bound to a driver** (D-36 — supersedes the "exactly one school" rule of CR-04 v≤0.12/D-07): matching allocates **individual runs**, and which school's students a driver serves is an output of each allocation. A driver **may serve multiple schools** across runs, provided every pair of same-day runs satisfies the compatibility rules (CR-26). Availability declarations never name a school (CR-03, D-35). |
| CR-05 | The administrator shall be able to trigger a matching run at any time via an explicit admin action. |
| CR-06 | Matching shall assign every registered student to a driver whenever a **feasible assignment exists** — defined per school, per shift, **and per direction run**: every school's students of a given shift can be covered for each direction by the drivers allocated to that school, respecting each driver's capacity (per direction, A-13), the same-day run-compatibility rules (CR-26 — non-overlap plus cross-school turnaround), the gender rule (CR-21), the service-area rule (CR-22), and the time rule (CR-23) for that direction's eligible driver pool. Flagged students (CR-19) are excluded from the count. Behavior when no feasible assignment exists: see CR-18 (independent review IR-01; retargeted per direction by review V4-02). |
| CR-07 | The optimization objective is **minimize total driving distance** — the *only* objective. No fairness or balance constraints apply. The objective applies **per direction run** (each to-school run and each from-school run, CR-08). Distance = **real road distance without traffic** (D-19); road-network data: self-hosted open-source routing engine (D-44; OQ-14(c) resolved). |
| CR-08 | **Shift/direction model (D-33, replaces the single-morning-run model):** each school shift has a **to-school run** (covers the shift start) and a **from-school run** (covers the shift end). Each direction run's driving distance is **evaluated separately** (minimize total distance per run, CR-07), and **to-school and from-school assignments are independent — a student may have a different driver per direction (D-32)**; assignment **decisions** across all runs are made in **one global optimization**, among whose distance-optimal plans a soft same-driver continuity preference applies (D-45) — it never overrides distance. The noon run is no longer defined as the reversed morning plan; it is the from-school run of the relevant shift. |
| CR-09 | Matching shall produce a **reviewable report**; the administrator reviews it before anything becomes final. |
| CR-10 | On approval, assignments become visible to parents and drivers via **both channels: dashboard and exported file** (standardized from "and/or" — independent review IR-15; consistent with D-18). |
| CR-11 | The administrator may tweak individual assignments without re-running matching; tweaks apply immediately on save. |
| CR-12 | Drivers shall receive, **per shift and per direction run**, the list of students with **sequence order and time information per student** — to-school: pickup order and pickup times; from-school: one school-departure time plus drop-off order and drop-off times (D-39) — via **both channels: dashboard and exported file** (consistent with D-18). Times are informational (A-07). |
| CR-13 | The system shall provide **no notifications** and require **no pickup confirmation/check-in** (explicitly out of scope). |
| CR-14 | Student absence: **no reporting mechanism** in the MVP (possible future feature). |
| CR-15 | Driver absence: **no system handling** — no backup pool, no automatic action (admin may intervene manually outside the system). |
| CR-16 | Mid-year changes (new student, student moves, student leaves): the administrator updates the registry and may tweak assignments manually; **matching is not re-run**, **except an admin-triggered capacity re-validation run (D-23)**; no day-of edits by the administrator. |
| CR-17 | Scale target for MVP: **hundreds of students**, coverage of **one city**, mixed driver capacities. |
| CR-18 | When no feasible assignment exists for a shift or direction (OQ-01, resolved), matching shall **not** produce a partial plan for publication; it shall instead report the failure with complete data — the list of unassigned students, the **shortfall per school/shift/direction** (including gender, area, and time causes), and the blocked assignments. **Remedies must respect eligibility (V4-11):** added drivers must be eligible for the failing group (gender/area/time); flagging students resolves demand-side failures (capacity/coverage) and does not resolve supply-side eligibility failures unless all affected students are flagged. The administrator resolves the failure by adding eligible drivers (then re-running) or by flagging students for exclusion (CR-19). |
| CR-19 | The administrator shall be able to **flag (disable) a student for assignment**; flagged students are excluded from matching runs (all shifts/directions) and are marked as flagged in reports. The flag is reversible. **Flag and removal propagate immediately (OQ-11 → D-50):** flagging or removing a student mid-year updates **live rosters (US-07/CR-12) and parent views (US-08/CR-10) at once**; matching is **not re-run** (CR-16/D-23 unchanged). |
| CR-20 | Parent/guardian accounts shall be created in one of two ways (OQ-04, resolved): **automatically from school-provided data**, or **via a parent registration request that becomes active when the administrator/school accepts it**. Only active accounts can view assignments. |
| CR-21 | **Gender rules (D-29, MOKEAB R-1/R-2):** a **male driver may transport only male students**; a **female driver may transport both male and female students**. A student's sex always equals their school's sex; therefore **all students in one service (car) are of the same sex** (car purity — MOKEAB R-3). |
| CR-22 | **Service-area rule (D-31, MOKEAB R-3-location):** a driver is eligible for a student group only if **both** the school's location **and every student's home location** lie inside the driver's service area (**one or more predefined zones**, stored and consumed as polygons — D-31/D-43). Only spatial coverage matters — not route or distance. |
| CR-23 | **Time rule (D-32, MOKEAB R-2-time):** a driver is eligible for a direction run only if the driver's service time window **strictly contains** the shift's relevant time for that direction — shift start (to-school) / shift end (from-school). No partial overlaps, no grace periods (MOKEAB strictness). **Scope-restricted by D-47 (2026-08-23):** at MVP the service window derives from the selected shift (D-42), so strict containment governs only **explicitly declared windows** (the deferred post-MVP sub-window feature); MVP eligibility is selection-based — see D-47. |
| CR-24 | **Direction independence (D-32, MOKEAB R-6):** a student's to-school and from-school assignments are independent; a driver selecting only one direction of a shift serves only that direction. |
| CR-25 | **Shift-catalog overlap policy (D-38):** shifts are system-defined entities — a start time and an end time — which schools adopt (each school has one or more, CR-01/D-33). Shifts may overlap arbitrarily (partial, nested, touching), both across the catalog and **within a single school's shift set**; no validation applies at definition time. Constraints bind only a driver's availability selections (US-03 AC-13/AC-14) and the runs matching allocates (CR-26). |
| CR-26 | **Same-day run compatibility / turnaround (D-37):** for any two runs allocated to the same driver on the same day, ordered in time (A ends ≤ B starts): **(i)** their clock windows must not overlap; **(ii)** if A and B serve **different schools**, the gap must satisfy B.S − A.E ≥ 2 × **MIN_TURNAROUND_MINUTES** — the earlier run's drop-off reserves until A.E + T and the later run's pickup preparation reserves from B.S − T, and the two reservations cannot merge across schools; **(iii)** consecutive runs at the **same school** require only (i) — back-to-back is valid. Conventions: half-open intervals [start, end) (A-16); selecting either direction of a shift reserves the shift's full buffered window [S − T, E + T] for that run (A-15). **MIN_TURNAROUND_MINUTES is system configuration; MVP value 30** (tunable per scenario post-MVP, not per-scenario in the MVP). Declaration-time checks: US-03 AC-13/AC-14; authoritative enforcement during matching feasibility. |

| D-42 | **Service-window derivation (OQ-19 resolved — product owner 2026-08-23):** at MVP the DriverShift service window **derives from the selected shift**; selecting a shift + direction makes the driver eligible for that direction with CR-23 evaluated against the shift's own start/end. | D-43 | **Service-area representation (OQ-16 resolved — product owner 2026-08-23):** drivers select **one or more predefined zones** (multi-select; stored and consumed as polygons). Center+radius entry mode is post-MVP. CR-03/CR-22 wording aligned; D-31 annotated. |
| D-44 | **Road-network data (OQ-14(c) resolved — product owner 2026-08-23):** distance/routing data comes from a **self-hosted open-source routing engine** (containerized deployment; engine selection and hardware sizing are Phase 3 design choices). No paid per-request routing/geocoding API; self-hosting keeps recurring cost inside the NFR-08 $30/month operating ceiling. Address/map entry remains per D-28. |
| D-45 | **Global optimum + deterministic tie-breaks (product owner 2026-08-23):** matching performs **one global optimization across all schools, shifts, and directions**, minimizing **total driving distance** (CR-07/D-21 unchanged). Competing demands for the same driver are resolved **exclusively by contribution to the global optimum** — no fairness quotas, no registration-order priority, no per-school reservations. The solver must be **deterministic**: identical registry input ⇒ identical report (required for reproducible reports and the AC-4 exhaustive verification). Among **distance-optimal** plans, ties break in order: (1) **maximum same-driver continuity** — the count of students whose to-school and from-school runs share one driver (the product owner's stated preference: same driver AM/PM whenever possible at zero distance cost); (2) stable lexicographic order of assignments (school, shift, direction, student). **Continuity never trades away distance**: if every optimal plan splits a student's directions, mixed drivers stand (CR-24).
